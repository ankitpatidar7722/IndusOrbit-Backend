Purchase Invoice

Definition

A purchase invoice is a document provided by a seller to a buyer, detailing a transaction for goods. It includes information such as the invoice number, date, seller and buyer details, a description of items, total amount, and any applicable taxes.

Steps

  1. Select Your Items (Same
  2. Click on the Create
  3. Select Purchase Ledger
  4. Verify invoice Quantity and Rate,
  5. Select the Additional Charges
  6. Enter Bill Number
  7. Click on the Save button
  8. Click on the Yes button
