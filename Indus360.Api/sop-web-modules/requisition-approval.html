Requisition Approval

Definition

Requisition approval in the printing industry is the process of authorizing a request for materials or services needed for a print job, ensuring they are necessary and available before procurement or issuance.

Steps

  1. Search and Select Items
  2. Click on the Requisition Detail
  3. Click on the Approve Button
  4. Click on the Yes Button
