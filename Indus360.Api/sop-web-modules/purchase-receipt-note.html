Purchase Receipt Note

Definition

A Purchase Receipt Note is a physical document that acknowledges the acceptance of goods. Serves as proof of the transaction, detailing essential information such as received items, quantity, date, and transporter details

Steps

  1. Select the received
  2. Click on the Next button
  3. Enter the Received
  4. Enter the Supplier's
  5. Enter the Manufacturing and
  6. Click here to open Select
  7. Select Warehouse and Bin (It
  8. Click on the Apply button
  9. Enter Invoice/
  10. Enter Gate Entry Number
  11. Enter Vehicle Number
  12. Enter Transporter Name
  13. Select Material Recipient Name
  14. Click on the Save button
  15. Click on the Yes button
