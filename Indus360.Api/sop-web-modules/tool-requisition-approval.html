Tool Requisition Approval

Definition

Tool Requisition approval in the printing industry is the process of authorizing a request for materials or services needed for a print job, ensuring they are necessary and available before procurement or issuance.

Steps

  1. Search and Select Items
  2. Click on the Approve Button
  3. Click on the Yes Button
